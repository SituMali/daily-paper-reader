## 摘要
中介分析越来越多地应用于随机试验中，但足以检验总处理效应的样本量可能导致自然间接效应或自然直接效应严重检验效能不足。随机化并不延伸至中介变量，因此精度取决于条件中介分布和中介-结局关联，而这二者均不纳入总效应计算。在线性结构方程模型之外的规划主要基于在完全指定的数据生成机制下进行模拟，而这种机制在设计阶段很少可得。本文针对二元中介变量和连续或二元结局，开发了用于自然间接效应和自然直接效应的解析检验效能及样本量公式。在标准识别假设下，我们聚焦于无需结局模型即可进行效应估计的中介概率比加权估计量。我们将该估计量的神谕方差分解为捕捉中介概率比变异性、结局变异及其关联的组成部分，并为自然间接效应额外添加一个共享臂协方差项。在概率比潜指标中介和工作结局模型下，这些组成部分由少量可解释的设计输入决定，而非由协变量、中介和结局的完整联合分布决定。模拟表明，解析样本量与基于模拟的基准值紧密匹配，达到目标检验效能，并将第一类错误维持在名义水平附近。一个ACTG175示例展示了如何利用预试验数据校准输入参数。

## Abstract
Mediation analyses are increasingly conducted in randomized trials, but a sample size adequate for the total treatment effect may leave the natural indirect effect (NIE) or natural direct effect (NDE) substantially underpowered. Randomization does not extend to the mediator, so precision depends on the conditional mediator distribution and the mediator-outcome association, neither of which enters a total-effect calculation. Planning outside linear structural equation models is largely based on simulation under a fully specified data-generating mechanism rarely available at the design stage. This paper develops analytic power and sample size formulas for the NIE and NDE with a binary mediator and a continuous or binary outcome. Under standard identification assumptions, we focus on the ratio-of-mediator-probability weighting (RMPW) estimator that does not require an outcome model for effect estimation. We decompose the oracle variances of the RMPW estimators into components capturing mediator-probability-ratio variability, outcome variation, and their association, with an additional shared-arm covariance term for the NIE. Under a probit latent-index mediator and a working outcome model, these components are determined by a small number of interpretable design inputs rather than by the full joint distribution of covariates, mediator, and outcome. Simulations show that the analytic sample sizes closely match simulation-based benchmarks, attain the target power, and maintain type I error near the nominal level. An ACTG175 illustration shows how pilot data can calibrate the inputs.
